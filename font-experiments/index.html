Randomize Studio

A playground for exploring typography
The green room where type tries on outfits before the big show. Pick a face for the title, a quieter one for the body, and let the dice handle the awkward first dates. Built for portfolio covers, case-study headers, album sleeves, and the hero section you'll secretly redesign at midnight. Every font streams live from Google Fonts, so you can flirt with Playfair, commit to Inter, and install nothing. When a pairing finally clicks — and it will — copy the CSS and walk away as though you planned the whole performance.
Edit
  • Click a block to select it — its section opens in the panel
  • Drag to place it, double-click to edit its text
  • Corner handles resize the box the text flows in
  • Ctrl-click adds to the selection; they move together
  • ←↑↓→ nudge by one, with Shift by ten
  • R randomizes, Esc deselects
  • Each block owns its colour, shadow and box; a plate sits behind them
  • Lock a section to keep its type, size and placement through a re-roll
